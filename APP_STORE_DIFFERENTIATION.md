# KittyMart vs PuppyMart: App Store Differentiation

## A. Why KittyMart is a distinct app

| Area | PuppyMart | KittyMart |
|---|---|---|
| Theme | Dogs, bright outdoor play | Cats, a cosy night-time market shop |
| Core mechanic | Timing-bar catch, then breeding | Stealth befriending: hold to creep while the cat grooms, freeze when it watches. A cat's "tell" (ear twitch) warns you before it looks |
| Secondary systems | Breeding, racing, dress-up room, diary, leaderboard | Running a shop: serve customers, assign cats to jobs (greeter, window sunbather, shelf napper, floor mouser), keep moods up with treats |
| Win condition | Collect and breed breeds, race, climb the leaderboard | Grow a shop and win shy cats over, with fewer rewards for speed and more for patience |
| Art style and palette | Pastel, Animal Crossing-style, pink and teal | Deep indigo and amber lantern light, neon sign, front-facing sitting cats |
| Currency | DoggyDollars | KittyCoins |
| Progression | Collection and breeding | Shop upgrades (stock, neon sign, heated floor, perches), unlockable districts (Back Alley, Fish Pier, Rooftops) with rarer coats |
| Characters | Side-view dogs | Original cat coats (ginger, smoky, shadow, cloud, tuxedo, calico, Siamese, Bengal, Starry Night) |
| UI | Pink pill buttons, round side buttons | Dark theme, amber and teal accents, bottom nav bar, single "hold to creep" control |
| Name, icon, screenshots | Dog-based | Cat shopkeeper icon, night-market screenshots, no shared artwork |

Shared with PuppyMart only: tap/touch control style and the general collect-and-care idea. Gameplay, art, audio feel and economy are all separate.

## B. App Store listing draft

- **Name:** KittyMart
- **Subtitle (26 chars):** Cozy cat night market shop
- **Category:** Games > Simulation (secondary: Casual)
- **Keywords (under 100 chars):** cat,kitten,shop,cozy,stealth,collect,market,pets,idle,cute,night,stray,café,relax,tycoon
- **Age rating notes:** No violence, no gambling, no user-generated content, no in-app purchases in v1. Suggested rating 4+. Re-check the questionnaire at submission.

**Description**

Open the lanterns on your very own night market. Customers line up, the neon sign hums, and your shop cats are on the job.

Sneak out on a Night Stroll to meet shy strays. Creep closer while a cat grooms, and freeze the moment it turns to watch you. Win its trust with a fish treat and a new coworker joins your shop.

- Befriend 9 cat coats, from Ginger Tabby to the rare Starry Night
- Give each cat a job: greeter, sunbather, napper or mouser
- Keep moods up with treats and pets
- Upgrade stock, neon sign and heated floor
- Unlock the Fish Pier and Rooftops for rarer cats
- Plays offline and saves on your device

**What's New (1.0):** Welcome to KittyMart! Open your shop and meet your first stray.

**Review notes (Guideline 4.3 spam/duplicate concerns)**

Apple's guideline on duplicate or "spam" apps is aimed at repackaged copies of the same app from one developer. KittyMart is not a reskin of our other game, PuppyMart. Please note:
1. The core loop is different. PuppyMart uses a timing-bar catch; KittyMart uses a stealth mechanic with a warning "tell" and an alert meter.
2. KittyMart adds shop management and cat job assignment, which PuppyMart does not have.
3. All art, names, currency, palette, UI and audio are original and separate.
4. Progression is different: shop upgrades and district unlocks instead of breeding and racing.
5. It is a standalone game with its own save data, bundle ID and screenshots.

We can provide a build comparison or video on request.

## C. Fallback if rejected under 4.3

Ship KittyMart as an expansion pack inside PuppyMart instead of a separate app:
- Add a "KittyMart" entry in the PuppyMart menu that opens the cat shop and Night Stroll scenes.
- Keep PuppyMart's save and account system; store cat data alongside dogs and add a KittyCoins balance.
- Remove the separate icon, listing and bundle ID. Move the keywords and description text above into PuppyMart's "What's New".
- Keep the stealth mechanic and shop management exactly as built, since they are the differentiators.
- Tell existing PuppyMart players through the update notes and a one-time in-app banner.

Note: the memory-recorded preference was a separate app with its own link; the expansion route is only the backup plan.
